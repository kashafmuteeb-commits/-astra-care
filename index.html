NASA Research Library

Research sources that can inform the design of astronaut health monitoring systems.

Research data and simulated dashboard readings are different things. ASTRA CARE does not claim that the values shown in its simulator came from NASA.

01 / NASA Open Science Data Repository

NASA's Open Science Data Repository provides access to biological and physical research connected to spaceflight and spaceflight analog studies.

Open NASA OSDR → Explore OSDR data →

02 / Inspiration4 Research: OSD-569

OSD-569 is a NASA repository study associated with whole-blood measurements from the Inspiration4 mission. The study includes biological measurements taken around the mission, rather than continuous heart-rate or blood-pressure telemetry.

Important limitation: this type of research dataset should not be presented as a real-time vital-sign feed.

Find OSD-569 in NASA OSDR →

03 / NASA Human Research Program

NASA's Human Research Program studies risks to astronaut health during space exploration, including changes related to altered gravity, radiation, isolation and other spaceflight hazards.

NASA HRP: Risks →

04 / NASA Life Sciences Portal

A resource for discovering life-sciences research and datasets related to human spaceflight.

NASA Life Sciences Data Archive →

Research Integration Plan

  1. Identify a relevant NASA study and its study ID.
  2. Record the variables, collection dates and study limitations.
  3. Use only the measurements actually supplied by that study.
  4. Keep synthetic mission telemetry in a separate dataset.
  5. Cite the source beside every research-derived result.

Data Management

Records are stored in local browser storage. They do not automatically synchronize between devices or upload to NASA.

Project Limitations

  • Not clinically validated or approved for medical use.
  • No live connection to spacecraft sensors.
  • No live NASA API integration in this version.
  • Simulated readings are not real astronaut data.
  • Pattern rules are demonstrative and not flight-certified.
  • Browser storage may be lost if local site data is cleared.
